## What it does

Hotspots lets editors place up to 30 ordered points over a local image for product features, venue plans, diagrams, and annotated photography. Each point can contain detail or open a registered lazy target.

## Do I need to do anything?

Add the widget to a page in Layout Builder, complete its fields, and preview the public page before publishing.

## Where it shows up

In Layout Builder, as a Hotspots widget with numeric positions, labels, descriptions, and optional registered targets.

## Good to know

- This free beta extension belongs to Capell Foundation and requires Layout Builder.
- Hotspots are at least 44 pixels, keyboard reachable, and optional targets use encrypted snapshot locators instead of raw identifiers.
